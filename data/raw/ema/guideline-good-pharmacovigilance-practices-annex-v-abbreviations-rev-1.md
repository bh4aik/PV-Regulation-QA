Annex V – Abbreviations (Rev 1*)
* Revision 1 includes a revision to the abbreviation SDR and the following additional abbreviations:
Add, ADME, aRMM, BSA, CAT, DCP, EDI, EMA, Enpr-EMA, eRMR, EudraCT, EU-POG, EV EWP, GPG,
HLGT, ID, ME, MRP, No, PDCO, PEM, PhV, PIP, PUMA, RCT, RMM, SCOPE and US FDA.
